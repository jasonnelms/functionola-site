GOOD QUESTIONS

Let's clear
the crumbs.

Everything you need to know about this concept shop.

Can I actually order FunctioNola?

Not yet! This is an interactive storefront demonstration for a college business project. You can browse, add items to your bag, and complete a simulated checkout, but no products are sold or shipped.

Are the product prices final?

No. All prices and shipping offers on this website are illustrative examples created for the business project.

What about ingredients and nutrition?

The packaging artwork represents the product concept. Ingredient, allergen, nutrition, and wellness claims require confirmation and review before any real-world sale.

Will checkout charge my card?

Never. Our demo checkout does not ask for a card number or contact a payment processor. Checkout shows fictional, read-only details; nothing is submitted or stored.
